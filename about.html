I’m an Information Systems student at Kyambogo University with a growing interest in building practical, database-driven web systems. Most of my hands-on experience so far comes from projects where clear workflows and accurate records matter, especially around stock, dispatch, and reporting.

During my internship at LAPOK Ventures, I worked on Outpost DMS and a product catalogue site. Outpost DMS is a live PHP/MySQL system for multi-depot distribution tasks such as stock taking, dispatch based on approved assignments, cash handover, and PDF reporting. The catalogue site supports product presentation and WhatsApp-based ordering.

Since September 2026, I’ve been a sales intern at Mitsumi Distribution (Devjyot Traders) in Kampala. My focus there is learning Veeam’s data-protection portfolio, including backup and recovery concepts, licensing basics, and how to communicate technical value clearly.

My current strengths are in PHP, MySQL, SQL design, and front-end fundamentals. I’m also building experience with JavaScript, Next.js, and Node.js, and I’m looking for junior developer opportunities where I can keep learning while contributing to real systems.
