About me

Hi, I'm Emmanuel

Software developer. Information Systems student at Kyambogo University.

See the work Résumé

I got into tech through an observation that wouldn't leave me alone: the businesses around me run on paper, guesswork and overworked spreadsheets — and nobody seems to find that strange. I find it strange. So I learned to build the alternative.

Today my work runs in real operations: a multi-depot distribution platform used every working day by depot staff, and a catalogue site taking real orders — both shipped during my internship at LAPOK Ventures, where I also stood in front of Coca-Cola executives and presented what I'd built.

Since September 2026 I've been a presales intern at Mitsumi Distribution (Devjyot Traders) in Kampala, working the Veeam data-protection portfolio — building campaign material, mapping the vendor line card, and learning the licensing and recovery economics behind enterprise backup.

What drives me is software where correctness matters — stock counts that balance, cash handovers that reconcile, permissions that actually hold. My foundation is PHP and MySQL; Next.js and Node.js are where I'm pushing next.
