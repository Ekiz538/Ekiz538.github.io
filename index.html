03 — About me

I'm a software developer studying Information Systems at Kyambogo University — and the person behind the distribution platform that LAPOK Ventures' depot staff rely on every working day.

Since my first database coursework I've been taking systems from classroom sketches to production: a PHP and MySQL backend that balances real cash handovers across depots, an e-commerce site whose customers order over WhatsApp, and invited contributions inside a professional team's Next.js and Node.js codebase.

What drives me is correctness — stock counts that balance, permissions that hold, reports leadership can act on. Right now I'm deepening that foundation with Linux administration and networking, because knowing how everything connects is half the job.
